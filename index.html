What is a Managed Intelligence Provider?

A Managed Intelligence Provider helps organizations operate AI like infrastructure: selected, configured, connected, maintained, and improved over time.

We work across models and platforms, so organizations are not locked into one vendor, one tool, or one way of working.

Services

Private AI Workspace

A controlled AI environment for your team.

Includes model access, user permissions, usage policies, and staff onboarding across hosted and open-weight models.

Company Knowledge System

A searchable knowledge layer for your organization.

Turns documents, policies, procedures, and internal reference material into usable working memory.

Workflow Automation

AI connected to real work.

Automates recurring work across forms, email, documents, CRMs, reports, and internal tools.

Managed Intelligence Support

Ongoing operation and improvement.

Includes monitoring, troubleshooting, evaluation, model changes, workflow updates, and monthly support.

Works across makes and models
